01

Lucent

My own streetwear brand · Drop 01 store

Last updated Sep 11, 2026

The drop site for Lucent, my streetwear brand. Pick a colorway and the whole page changes with it: colors, corners and the animated scene behind everything.

What it does

  • Nine colorway swatches swap the product photo and move the site into one of four hand-built parallax scenes: Concrete, Shore, Bloom and Meadow.
  • The starting colorway follows the visitor's local time of day.
  • Product page with stock per size, a three-question size finder, US/UK/EU/AU sizing and a side-by-side colorway compare.
  • Cart drawer with bundle pricing, gift wrap and a free-shipping progress bar.
  • A launch countdown that switched the site from pre-launch to pre-order on its own.

What the latest update added

A big expansion of the one-file store. In one day of uploads it grew from 776 lines to 1,924.

02

Essence of Childcare University

Client · Licensed childcare center, Milwaukee

Last updated Oct 7, 2026

A full site for a Milwaukee childcare center that is open until midnight. Every classroom has its own color, and the photos are of the real rooms and kids' activities.

What it does

  • Eleven pages, from Programs and Tuition to Transportation and Careers.
  • An hours strip that draws the 6 AM to 12 AM open window on a 24-hour bar.
  • A 27-photo gallery with category filters and a keyboard-friendly lightbox.
  • Enrollment, job and tour forms that never pretend to send. Until a form service is connected, parents get a pre-filled call, text or email instead.
  • A sticky Call, Tour and Enroll bar on phones, plus automatic dark mode.

What the latest update added

A Transportation page with round-trip and one-way pricing. Late September added the owner's own wording, a QR code to the parents' shared forms folder, and nine new photos.

03

Dawg City

Client · Hot dog stand at 3rd Street Market Hall

Last updated Oct 7, 2026

A dark, loud food site for the hot dog stand inside 3rd Street Market Hall, with the full menu and both ways to order.

What it does

  • A live open or closed badge worked out from the stand's real hours, with today's row highlighted.
  • A tabbed menu of 39 items, with prices matched to the live ordering page.
  • A pinned photo reel that slides sideways as you scroll on desktop and swipes on phones.
  • A counting loader into a letter-by-letter headline, plus signature cards with 3D tilt and glare.
  • Order Pickup opens me&u, Get Delivery opens DoorDash, and a map shows the West Hall spot.

What the latest update added

The full rebuild: loader, split-letter hero, depth buttons, tilt cards, pinned reel and map, with the real Boss Hogg and Hickory photos and menu prices matched to live ordering.

04

ken.didit

Client · Kenya's braids, locs and quick weaves

Last updated Oct 9, 2026

A booking site for Kenya's braiding business. Clients pick a style, see the price and time, choose an open day and pay the deposit in one pass.

What it does

  • 23 styles in 6 categories, each with its price and how long it takes. Tap one to start booking with it already picked.
  • A booking calendar that reads Kenya's own Google Sheet, with a backup schedule if the sheet can't load.
  • A live appointment slip with the estimated total, the $15 deposit and the balance due.
  • The deposit goes through Stripe (Apple Pay or card), then the request goes to Kenya by text or Instagram DM.
  • An 18-photo gallery where "Book this style" picks the matching service for you.

What the latest update added

The calendar now reads Kenya's Google Sheet and opens on the first month with open dates. Her October openings went in the same day.

05

Mar Latrice Styles

Client · Healthy-hair salon, West Allis

Last updated Oct 8, 2026

An editorial salon site built from her logo lockup, with every service, price, deposit and time on the page and booking through her Square.

What it does

  • A 33-service menu in 7 categories with price, deposit and time on every row. It works with JavaScript off.
  • Live search and category chips with a running count of matching services.
  • Tap any service for a detail sheet with a Book on Square button.
  • "How booking works" in four steps, written from her real deposit policy, plus her ten policies in plain words.
  • Seven of her Instagram posts shown uncropped, each linking to the original.

What the latest update added

The first full build: the logo-lockup design, her full Square menu, her real work and her policies.

06

Hidden Gems

My own web app · City guide

Last updated Oct 9, 2026

A city guide that finds local spots the guidebooks miss. It is styled like a field guide, and every spot card is a numbered luggage tag.

What it does

  • Pick a city by map pin, list or search, choose one of six vibes, and get six spots, each with a photo, a must-try and an insider tip.
  • A city panel with weather, local time, currency rate, daily budget and safety notes.
  • Saved places, local phrases, nearby cities and a printable 3-day itinerary.
  • API keys stay on the server. A locked-down Vercel proxy talks to the Anthropic and Pexels APIs with rate limits.

What the latest update added

A field-guide redesign with new loading, error and no-photo states, accessibility fixes and a hardened API proxy.

Open city-azure.vercel.app ↗

Tyrone Dunn · Issue Nº 04p.02 Owner Controls

Change your site without calling me

Some updates should not wait on a developer. Here is what owners already run themselves, and what I can set up next.

Live now

ken.didit

Kenya runs her booking calendar from a Google Sheet

She types her open dates and times into a sheet. Dropdowns keep each entry clean, and a how-to tab explains every column. The booking calendar on her site reads the sheet and updates itself. If the sheet can't be reached, a backup schedule built into the site takes over.

Mar Latrice Styles

Mar Latrice books through her own Square

Her availability, deposits and services live in her Square account. The site sends clients straight to Square for live open times, so changing a slot never needs me.

  • Open times: managed in Square
  • Deposits: collected by Square
  • Site menu: 33 services with deposits and times
