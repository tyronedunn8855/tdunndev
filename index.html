Essence of Childcare University

Client · Licensed childcare center, Milwaukee

Open essencechildcare.vercel.app Updated Oct 10, 2026

A licensed childcare center in Milwaukee, open from 6 AM to midnight. The site answers what parents ask before they call: rooms, tuition, meals, rides and how to enroll.

  • Eleven pages, from Programs and Tuition to Transportation and Careers.
  • An hours bar for the 6 AM to 12 AM window, plus an open-now status in Milwaukee time.
  • A color for every classroom, Infant to School-Age, and a meal strip for the food program.
  • 27 real photos with filters and a full-size viewer you work with the keyboard.
  • Enrollment, job and tour forms with error checks. Until a form service is connected, they hand parents a pre-filled text, call or email instead of faking a send.
  • 11 parent forms, plus a QR code to the center's shared Drive folder.
  • A Call, Tour and Enroll bar on phones, a page jump bar and automatic dark mode.

She runs: the parent documents. They live in the center's shared Google Drive folder, and the QR code on the site always opens the current files.

New families: parents compare centers at night. Tuition, hours and the tour form are one tap away on any phone.

Time saved: the fees table, transportation prices and parent FAQ answer common questions before anyone picks up the phone.

I make every change for her: new photos, her own wording on any card, new programs, fees or pages.

Recent updates: her wording on three cards, nine new photos, a Transportation page, and a speed pass to cut the first load on phones from about 5.6 MB to 0.5 MB.

When she opens a form service account, I connect the forms so they send straight to her inbox.

Dawg City

Client · Mr. Ken's hot dog stand, 3rd Street Market Hall

Open dawgcity.vercel.app Updated Oct 10, 2026

A specialty hot dog stand inside 3rd Street Market Hall in Milwaukee. The site sells the food first: big photos, the full menu and two buttons to order.

  • A live open or closed badge from the stand's real hours, in Milwaukee time, with today highlighted.
  • A 39-item menu in tabs, with prices matched to the live ordering page.
  • Order Pickup opens me&u. Get Delivery opens DoorDash.
  • A fixed Pickup, Delivery and Directions bar on phones.
  • A map of the West Hall spot, with an address card if the map fails to load.

He runs: online orders and item availability, in the me&u and DoorDash accounts he already uses.

New customers: someone searching on a phone sees open or closed, the menu and an order button within one screen.

Time saved: menu prices match live ordering, so the counter and the site never disagree on a price.

Menu items, prices and hours live in the site code, so I change them for him. Text me a new price, a new dawg or holiday hours and I push the update.

Recent updates: Chili Cheese on the signature cards, perch as a +$3.50 add-on, and a phone pass with lighter photos and a fixed order bar.

ken.didit

Client · Kenya's braids, locs and quick weaves

Open kenyastyles.vercel.app Updated Oct 10, 2026

Kenya braids hair and books clients through Instagram. The site turns a long DM thread into one booking flow: pick a style, see the price and time, pick an open day, pay the deposit.

  • 23 styles in 6 categories, each with its price and how long it takes.
  • A 3-step booker. Add-ons change both the price and the time estimate.
  • A calendar fed by Kenya's Google Sheet, with a backup schedule built in.
  • A live slip with the total, the $15 deposit and the balance due.
  • Deposit through Stripe (Apple Pay or card), then the request goes to Kenya by text or Instagram DM.
  • After sending, the client gets a calendar file and a Google Calendar link.
  • An 18-photo gallery where "Book this style" picks the service, and a plaited-k logo.

She runs: her open days and times. She types them into a Google Sheet and the calendar updates itself. Payments sit in her Stripe dashboard.

New clients: people book from the link in her bio at any hour, and they see the price and time before they ask.

Time saved: every request arrives with the style, add-ons, date, time and deposit status, so no back-and-forth to collect details.

I change prices, styles, times and gallery photos for her, and keep the sheet and the deposit link working.

Recent updates: her October openings, every photo upscaled and shown uncropped, a saved-progress booking flow and a new logo.

Mar Latrice Styles

Client · Healthy-hair salon, West Allis

Open mariahhairpage.vercel.app Updated Oct 10, 2026

A healthy-hair salon in West Allis. The site lists every service with its price, deposit and time, then sends clients to her Square to book.

  • 33 services in 7 categories, with price, deposit and time on every row. The menu works with JavaScript off.
  • Search and category chips with a running count of matches.
  • Tap a service for a detail sheet with a Book on Square button.
  • A live 3D silk press: a lock of hair goes from coiled to sleek as you scroll.
  • "How booking works" in four steps, and her ten policies in plain words.
  • Seven of her Instagram posts, uncropped, each linking to the original.

She runs: open times, deposits and services in her Square account. The site sends people to Square for live times, so a schedule change never waits on me.

New clients: first-timers get a clear "start here" service and the full menu before they book, so they pick the right appointment.

Time saved: deposits, the 48-hour cancel rule and the late fee sit on the page, so clients read the rules before they book.

When she adds or reprices a service in Square, I update the menu on the site to match.

New work for the gallery and any policy change go in the same way: send it, and I post it.

Lucent

My own streetwear brand · Drop 01 store

Open testbrand-ten.vercel.app Updated Sep 11, 2026

Lucent is my streetwear brand. Pick a colorway in the Drop 01 store and the whole page changes with it: colors, corners and the scene behind everything.

  • Nine colorways move the site through four hand-built scenes: Concrete, Shore, Bloom and Meadow.
  • The first colorway follows the visitor's time of day.
  • Stock per size, a three-question size finder, US/UK/EU/AU sizes and a side-by-side compare.
  • A cart with bundle pricing, gift wrap and a free-shipping bar.
  • A countdown switched the store from pre-launch to pre-order on its own.
  • Five currencies, light and dark mode, and a sound toggle.

An admin page edits price and stock per size and exports the numbers as a file.

Checkout and email signups go live once Stripe and a mail service are connected. Until then the store says so instead of faking an order.

One file, no build step. New drops, colorways, prices and stock go in through one settings block.

In one day of updates the store grew from 776 lines to 1,924.

Hidden Gems

My own web app · City guide

Open city-azure.vercel.app Updated Oct 9, 2026

A city guide for the local spots guidebooks miss. It looks like a field guide, and every spot card is a numbered luggage tag.

  • Pick a city by map pin, list or search, then one of six vibes.
  • Six spots per search, each with a photo, a must-try and an insider tip.
  • A city panel with weather, local time, currency rate, daily budget and safety notes.
  • Saved places, local phrases, nearby cities and a printable 3-day plan.

Each search goes to a small server function on Vercel. The function asks Claude for the picks and Pexels for the photos.

Loading, error and no-photo states are designed too, so a slow answer never looks broken.

API keys stay on the server. The proxy only takes this app's requests from this site, caps size and length, and rate-limits each visitor.

Text from the AI is escaped before it reaches the page.
